# Cinematic design direction

Carry forward the original workflow's asymmetric composition, moderate motion, and spacious layout. These are defaults: the user's brand, reference image, and explicit preferences control the final direction.

## Composition

Let the image or video carry the atmosphere. Use left-aligned content, a strong focal point, intentional negative space, and a constrained copy width. On mobile, simplify to a single column and choose a crop that preserves the subject. Avoid extra cards, sections, badges, or navigation that do not serve the brief.

Make the hero fill the viewport where practical without forcing content to fit. Enlarged text and short screens must retain all content and controls.

## Typography, palette, and copy

Choose a typeface with a deliberate role: Geist or Outfit are useful defaults. Brand-provided fonts and a supplied reference can override them. Use size, weight, spacing, and line breaks to establish hierarchy; avoid oversized headings that collide with mobile controls.

Use an off-black base, readable near-white type, and one intentional accent by default. Match warm or cool neutrals consistently. Possible directions: amber with dark stone for warmth, restrained blue with slate for technology, or muted green with charcoal for nature. Avoid generic neon gradients and glow effects unless the reference calls for them.

Write specific benefits and clear actions. Do not fabricate customer counts, testimonials, awards, addresses, or product claims. A short headline and one or two useful supporting sentences usually suit the composition.

## Motion and finish

The video supplies the principal motion. Use restrained transform/opacity feedback for actual controls; a spring is optional, not a reason to animate every element. Match hover, active, focus, and disabled states. Reduced-motion users get a coherent still composition with no nonessential animation.

Judge contrast across moving frames, not just a thumbnail. Refine overlay direction, spacing, mobile crop, and focus visibility from the rendered page. Use a real poster instead of an arbitrary remote placeholder image. Follow [the implementation skill](../cinematic-site-builder/SKILL.md) for playback and accessibility behavior.
